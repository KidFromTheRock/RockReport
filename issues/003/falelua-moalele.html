Fale Moalele’s Development Move

Fale Moalele is one of the KFTR athletes we’re proud to work with, and his next chapter is exactly the kind of development story families should pay attention to.

Fale’s path started in Tafuna, American Samoa. Moalele is a defensive lineman from Tafuna, American Samoa, who spent his freshman through junior seasons at Tafuna High School before finishing his high school career at Liberty High School in Las Vegas.

Now, Fale is making another move: from Hawai‘i to Iowa Western Community College, the defending junior college national champions, where the goal is more exposure, more playing time and the next stage of his development.

That development is already showing up physically. Since beginning this next phase, Fale has grown 2.25 inches and added 45 pounds — the kind of change that can reshape how a player is evaluated and where he fits on the field.

It takes a village

Keiki’s post on Fale’s move captured the bigger point: everyone deserves a second chance, and sometimes the right path requires faith, humility, hard work and gratitude.

That post also thanked Tafuna head coach Oakland Salave‘a for continuing to invest in his former student-athlete beyond the football field. Long after the games are over, that kind of coaching relationship can help shape the next opportunity.

For Fale, the move to Iowa is not just a transfer. It is a fresh opportunity to keep pursuing his academic and athletic goals while carrying the village with him.

The short view: Fale’s journey shows why development matters. Sometimes the right move is the one that creates more reps, more visibility and more room to grow.
